What to do about it

BI & Forecast

Fix the holiday calendar. The peak is the 2 weeks before Christmas, not the flagged week.

Store Ops

Staff and stock up for Thanksgiving–Dec 24. Treat Super Bowl as a normal week.

Ops & HR

Plan for January, 12% below average: clearance and lighter schedules.

Regional

Review Store 14 (−$7.3M) and Store 36 (−17.5%). Learn from 38, 44, 39.

Supply Chain

Allocate holiday stock per store. Peaks range from 1.0x to 2.05x.
